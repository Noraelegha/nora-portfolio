Section 02
Target Audiences

The campaign was tailored to three distinct audience segments with separate messaging and prizes for each.

Homeowners

People who own or rent a home and are interested in home security or solar panel solutions.

Prize: Free home security + solar consultation

Business Owners

People who own or rent a physical outlet and need business asset protection and sustainability advice.

Prize: Free security audit + sustainability consultation

Property Managers

People involved in furnishing or managing properties who need scalable security and eco solutions.

Prize: Discounted security install + sustainability consultation
Section 03
How It Worked
1

Sign Up

Participants visited the bio link and signed up via Google Form to receive their unique referral link.

2

Refer

Shared their unique referral link with friends, family, and colleagues who might be interested in home security, solar panels, or both.

3

Earn Entries

Every successful referral earned an entry into the contest. More referrals meant better odds of winning.

4

Engage

Participants also shared, liked, and commented on the campaign post — driving organic reach and algorithm visibility beyond the initial audience.

Section 06
What Made It Work

The Mechanic Was the Strategy

Most brands run giveaways that generate temporary spikes with no retention. This campaign worked because the referral mechanic was tied to genuine audience alignment — each participant had to actively recruit people who matched one of the three audience segments. That meant new followers arrived already interested in what the brand offers, not just chasing a prize. The result was a follower base that actually belonged there.

The Transferable Lesson

Organic community-driven growth can outperform paid campaigns when the incentive structure is designed around the right audience, not just the biggest prize. A $0 campaign that brings in the right 320 followers is worth more than a $500 campaign that brings in 3,000 who never engage again.
