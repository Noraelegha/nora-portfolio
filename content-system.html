Section 06
Sample Content Hooks
Ops Intelligence
LinkedIn Text Post · Monday
"Most ops teams don't have a capacity problem. They have a visibility problem."
Opens with a reframe. Explains how most bottlenecks come from unclear ownership, not headcount. Ends with a question that drives comments.
Client Win
Carousel · Wednesday
"We helped a 30-person ops team reclaim 12 hours a week. Here's exactly how."
Slide-by-slide breakdown of the problem, the change made, and the measurable outcome. Final slide: soft CTA to book a demo.
Short-Form Video
LinkedIn Video / Reel · Friday
"The sign your ops team is underwater (and it's not what you think)"
Talking-head format. 45-60 seconds. One insight, one example, one takeaway. No product pitch — pure value that drives organic reach.
Founder Voice
Text Post · Sunday
"We almost built the wrong product. This one conversation saved us."
Honest founder story. Connects to the audience's fear of building something nobody wants. Humanises the brand. High share rate.
