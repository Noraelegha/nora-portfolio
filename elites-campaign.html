Campaign Strategy Brief Strategy Sample

Instagram Ad Campaign
The Elites

A full Instagram paid campaign strategy built for a premium matchmaking brand targeting high-net-worth individuals across 7 global cities. Covers audience targeting, messaging framework, placement strategy, and budget allocation.

Section 01
Campaign Overview

The Elites — Premium Matchmaking Service

Instagram (Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels)

Paid — Brand Awareness + Lead Generation

$2,500+ per client

Capture high-quality leads via Instagram lead generation forms

Build brand recognition among affluent singles globally

Section 02
Campaign Goals

Primary — Lead Generation (80%)

  • Capture qualified leads via Instagram lead forms
  • Target individuals who can afford $2,500+ subscriptions
  • Drive consultation booking requests
  • Build a retargeting pool of warm prospects

Secondary — Brand Awareness (20%)

  • Establish The Elites as a premium authority
  • Reach affluent singles across 7 target cities
  • Position the brand alongside luxury lifestyle content
  • Build long-term brand recall for future conversions
Section 03
Target Audience

Demographic Targeting

30-50 years — mature, financially stable audience

London, New York, Los Angeles, Dubai, Johannesburg, Lagos, Paris

Luxury brands (Chanel, Rolex, Louis Vuitton), high-end travel, luxury real estate, fine dining, professional networking

Frequent international travellers, users who engage with luxury content, demonstrated spending on premium goods

Rationale: The campaign targets proven luxury consumers rather than aspirational audiences — ensuring leads arrive pre-qualified by income and lifestyle signals.

Section 04
Core Messaging Framework

Exclusivity

Position The Elites as a premium matchmaking service for affluent, successful individuals seeking meaningful connections — not mass-market dating.

Luxury

Highlight the luxurious, high-end nature of the service. Emphasise the benefits of a personalised, discreet matchmaking process that respects the client's time and privacy.

Success Stories

Showcase testimonials and stories from successful couples to build trust and demonstrate real outcomes — making the service aspirational and credible simultaneously.

Section 05
Ad Placements

Feed

Static images and videos for maximum visibility in core browsing flow

Stories

Short immersive video ads with swipe-up lead form

Explore

Discovery-phase ads reaching users actively looking for new content

Reels

Short-form video ads to capture attention and drive lead form completions

Section 06
Budget Allocation

80% Lead Generation

20%

Lead Generation — primary spend Brand Awareness

80/20 split prioritises measurable lead capture over brand impressions. Given the high subscription value ($2,500+), even a small number of quality leads justifies significant spend — the campaign is optimised for lead quality, not volume.

Section 07
Campaign Status and Strategic Notes

Status: Strategy Developed — Pending Execution Data

This brief was developed as a full campaign strategy for The Elites. The targeting framework, messaging architecture, placement mix, and budget logic were built out in full. Execution data is pending. This document is included as a demonstration of strategic campaign planning — showing the thinking that precedes any paid campaign, not just the results after it runs.

Why This Approach Works

Most agencies targeting luxury brands default to aesthetic-first creative. This strategy prioritises audience qualification first — using interest and behaviour signals that correlate with actual purchasing power, not just aspirational interest. The 80/20 budget split reflects a business reality: for a $2,500+ service, one converted lead pays back the entire campaign spend many times over. Every targeting and messaging decision was made with that math in mind.
