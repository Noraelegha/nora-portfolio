Case Study · Personal Brand

LinkedIn Personal Branding

I ran my own LinkedIn like a client account: one positioning, one content system, no ad spend. Here is what I did and what it produced.

5.5Kfollowers, up from 1,139
143Kimpressions on my best single post

What I did

01 · Positioning

Shifted from tips to beliefs

Moved away from generic social media advice and built content around challenging common industry assumptions, aimed at founders.

02 · Pillars

Four content pillars

  • Visibility vs Authority: why empty reach is not influence.
  • Content Strategy Misunderstandings: posting daily vs making strategic decisions.
  • Founder Thinking & Positioning: how leaders communicate, build trust, and market what they build.
  • Thought Leadership Mechanics: how perspective compounds into authority.
03 · Structure

One four-part post formula

  • Hook: a declarative opening, not an introduction.
  • Tension: challenge a familiar assumption.
  • Consequence: show what the argument means in practice.
  • Conversation: close to invite debate, built for comments and reposts over likes.
04 · Series

The Teardown Series

A recurring series where I walked through brands and founders already scaling in their field, broke down what they were doing, and showed how others could replicate it. Built to demonstrate working knowledge, not just opinions.

05 · Iteration

Data-led angle rotation

Reviewed analytics regularly and rotated angles based on what drove discussion and profile visits.

Campaign · Executed · Zero Ad Spend

Sandbox Referral Campaign

A community for freelancers and entrepreneurs. The account had 79 followers. I designed and ran a referral contest from scratch — in 3 days it reached 400+ followers, 937 comments, and zero naira spent on ads.

79 → 400+followers in 3 days
937comments generated
$0ad spend

The Brief

Challenge

Brand new account, no audience

Sandbox was an early-stage community brand. With 79 followers and no budget for paid ads, the goal was to grow fast using only organic mechanics — and make the growth stick.

Mechanic

Referral contest with social proof built in

Participants were asked to refer, share, and tag people to follow the brand. The person with the most referrals won a prize. Every participant had a personal incentive to recruit as many people as possible — the contest ran itself.

Why it worked

Community mechanics, not broadcast

Instead of pushing content out, the campaign activated existing followers to pull people in. Each participant became a distribution channel. The comment section became social proof — 937 comments showed activity, which attracted more followers.

Results

Follower growth: 79 → 400+ in 3 days

Duration: 3 days Ad spend: $0 Comments: 937

Growth of 400%+ in 72 hours with no paid distribution. The campaign also generated a comment volume that most accounts with 5× the followers would not see on a single post.

What this demonstrates: I can build and execute campaigns that generate outsized results from small audiences. The mechanic scales — the same referral structure was used in a second campaign (135 → 700+ followers in under 5 days) with consistent results.

Campaign Brief · Instagram Ads · Strategy

The Elites — Instagram Ad Strategy

A full paid campaign brief for a premium matchmaking brand targeting high-net-worth singles across 7 global cities. Subscription price: $2,500+. Brief written and approved; execution pending client go-ahead.

$2,500+subscription value per client
7 citiesLondon, NY, LA, Dubai, Joburg, Lagos, Paris
80 / 20lead gen vs brand awareness budget split

Campaign Objective

Primary Goal

High-quality lead generation

Capture inbound leads from affluent singles (30–50) who can afford premium matchmaking services. Using Instagram's native lead gen forms to reduce friction between interest and contact.

Secondary Goal

Brand positioning at the luxury tier

Establish The Elites as the premium, discreet choice in a crowded matchmaking market — not a dating app, but a personalised service for serious individuals.

Target Audience

Audience Profile

Age: 30–50 Tier: High-net-worth Locations: 7 global cities

Interests: luxury brands, high-end travel, fine dining, exclusive professional networks. Behaviours: frequent international travel, demonstrated luxury spend patterns. The targeting is built to filter out price-sensitive users from the first impression.

Ad Strategy

Core Messaging

Exclusivity · Discretion · Results

Three messaging pillars: position the service as exclusive (not for everyone), highlight the personalised and discreet process, and use real success stories as proof. No hard sell — let the quality speak.

Placements

Instagram Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels

Feed for sustained visibility. Stories with swipe-up for direct action. Explore for cold audience discovery. Reels for reach and emotional engagement — short-form video that captures attention before introducing the brand.

Budget Split

80% lead gen, 20% brand awareness

The majority of spend goes toward campaigns with a measurable conversion: a form fill, a DM, a booking request. Awareness spend seeds the audience for retargeting — it does not carry the lead gen burden alone.

Note: This brief was written and presented to the client. Execution is pending client approval and budget confirmation. It demonstrates campaign planning, paid media strategy, and audience architecture — not managed ad spend.

Content System Framework

Brand Snapshot — Arcflow

B2B Workflow Automation SaaS

Stage: Seed-funded · 12-person team Audience: Ops Managers, COOs, Founders Goal: Demo bookings from content

Primary platform: LinkedIn (decision-makers). Secondary: Instagram (brand awareness). Company size: 20–200 person scaling startups. The content goal is to position the founder as a trusted voice in ops and drive inbound demo requests.

The Four Content Pillars

Pillar 01 · Authority

Ops Intelligence

Tactical insights on workflow bottlenecks, team efficiency, and systems thinking. Shows expertise without pitching. Goal: build trust with the decision-maker before they're in buying mode.

Pillar 02 · Social Proof

Client Wins

Real results from customers — time saved, errors reduced, team capacity freed. Data-led storytelling that lets outcomes speak louder than features.

Pillar 03 · Founder Voice

Behind the Build

Transparent founder content — decisions made, lessons learned, what scaling actually looks like inside. Humanises the brand and builds a following that feels invested in the outcome.

Pillar 04 · Conversion

The Problem Frame

Content that names the exact pain the audience feels and positions Arcflow as the natural next step. Direct CTA. Used sparingly so it lands when it appears.

Format Mix & Cadence

Weekly posting: 3–4 times

Text posts: 40% Carousels: 25% Short-form video: 25% Static graphics: 10%

Monday: Ops Intelligence text post · Wednesday: Client Win carousel · Friday: Short-form video · Sunday: Founder Voice post. Each post type serves a specific funnel stage. The schedule is reviewed and adjusted after 30 days based on what platform data shows.

90-Day Success Metrics

Follower Growth

+500 net new LinkedIn followers

Organic growth from a consistent, audience-first content strategy. No paid follower acquisition.

Engagement Rate

3%+ average across all posts

Meaningful engagement — comments, shares, saves — not just passive impressions. Tracked monthly and used to inform format decisions.

Demo Inquiries

5–8 inbound demo requests from content

The business outcome that justifies the content investment. Every KPI in this system is tied to a real business number, not a vanity metric.
